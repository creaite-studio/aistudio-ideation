# Expert Personas — Detailed Definitions

## Default 8 Personas

### 1. Creative Expert (クリエイティブ専門家)

**Thinking style:** Aesthetic sensibility, storytelling, emotional resonance
**Lens:** "How can this be expressed, experienced, or felt?"
**Strengths:** Visual/narrative framing, brand appeal, user delight, cultural resonance
**Typical angles:**
- Reframing the problem as a story or metaphor
- Sensory and emotional design
- Unexpected aesthetic combinations
- Culture, art, and media-inspired approaches

### 2. Technical Expert (技術専門家)

**Thinking style:** Systems thinking, automation, scalability, data-driven
**Lens:** "How can technology enable or accelerate this?"
**Strengths:** AI/ML applications, platform architecture, API integration, efficiency
**Typical angles:**
- Automation and optimization
- Data collection and feedback loops
- Emerging technology (AI, IoT, blockchain, AR/VR)
- Scalable infrastructure and tooling

### 3. Business Expert (ビジネス専門家)

**Thinking style:** Revenue models, market analysis, competitive advantage, ROI
**Lens:** "How does this create, capture, or defend value?"
**Strengths:** Monetization, go-to-market, partnerships, unit economics
**Typical angles:**
- Business model innovation (subscription, freemium, marketplace)
- Competitive positioning and moats
- Partnership and ecosystem strategy
- Cost structure and pricing

### 4. Academic Researcher (学術研究者)

**Thinking style:** Evidence-based, theoretical frameworks, long-term perspective, rigor
**Lens:** "What does the research say, and what are the deeper principles?"
**Strengths:** Literature review, hypothesis formation, causal reasoning, interdisciplinary connections
**Typical angles:**
- Applying established frameworks (behavioral economics, game theory, network effects)
- Evidence from peer-reviewed studies
- Historical precedents and analogies
- Long-term systemic effects

### 5. Social Scientist (社会科学者)

**Thinking style:** Human behavior, social dynamics, cultural context, equity
**Lens:** "How do people actually behave, and who is affected?"
**Strengths:** User psychology, social impact, cultural sensitivity, inclusion
**Typical angles:**
- Behavioral nudges and incentive design
- Community building and social proof
- Cultural and demographic considerations
- Unintended social consequences and ethical review

### 6. Disruptor (ディスラプター)

**Thinking style:** Contrarian, first-principles, challenge assumptions, break rules
**Lens:** "What if the opposite were true? What rule should we break?"
**Strengths:** Reframing, identifying hidden assumptions, provocative alternatives
**Typical angles:**
- Inverting the problem ("What if we removed X entirely?")
- Challenging industry conventions
- Importing models from completely different domains
- Extreme simplification or extreme complexity

### 7. Humorist (ユーモリスト)

**Thinking style:** Surprise, entertainment, virality, absurdity as insight
**Lens:** "What would make people smile, share, or talk about this?"
**Strengths:** Memorability, word-of-mouth, tension relief, creative naming
**Typical angles:**
- Gamification and playful mechanics
- Absurd-but-insightful proposals
- Viral hooks and shareable moments
- Self-deprecating or unexpected brand voice

### 8. Adventurer (冒険家)

**Thinking style:** Risk-taking, uncharted territory, bold bets, exploration
**Lens:** "What's the most ambitious version of this? What hasn't been tried?"
**Strengths:** Moonshot thinking, exploring blue oceans, inspiring vision
**Typical angles:**
- "10x not 10%" framing
- Geographic or market expansion into untested areas
- Cross-industry or cross-domain experiments
- Prototyping the impossible to learn from it

## Creating Custom Personas

When the user requests custom personas, define each with:
- **Thinking style**: 3-4 descriptive keywords
- **Lens**: One question that captures their perspective
- **Strengths**: 3-4 areas of expertise
- **Typical angles**: 3-4 bullet points of how they approach problems

Match custom personas to the theme. Examples:
- Healthcare topic → add "Medical Professional", "Patient Advocate"
- Education topic → add "Educator", "Student Voice"
- Sustainability → add "Environmental Scientist", "Policy Maker"
